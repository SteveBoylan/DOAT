// Who we are

Local roots, nationwide reach

Dead or Alive Technology is a trusted, professional electronics repair service based in Wishaw, North Lanarkshire. In May 2025 we hit a new milestone — opening our first dedicated store, giving local customers a walk-in workshop backed by UK-wide collection, delivery and mail-in repair services.

Why replace when you can repair? Every device we revive is one less in landfill and money back in your pocket. From a single cracked screen to managing the IT for your whole business, we treat every job with the same care.
